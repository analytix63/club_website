Our Identity

Fueling Innovation Through Data & Creativity

ANALYTIX is the official Data Science, Analytics, Design, and Media community at MVJ College of Engineering. We bridge academic theory with real-world industry challenges.

Practical Analytics

We move beyond textbook concepts. Members work with real datasets, building machine learning models, predictive dashboards, and production web applications.

Hands-on Focus

Design & Media Synergy

Data requires compelling storytelling. Our Design and Content teams work side-by-side with developers to create intuitive interfaces and impactful media outreach.

Cross-Functional

Competitive Spirit

We regularly organize inter-departmental hackathons, code jams, and analytics leagues. We mentor members to compete in national-level engineering challenges.
